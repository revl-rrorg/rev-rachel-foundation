In focus / TUMO at 15

The confidence to say,
“I can build that.”

TUMO gives young people the freedom to follow their curiosity and discover what they can create. Born in Armenia, that idea now reaches young people around the world.

Its 15th anniversary gala celebrated that work and honored Vahe Kuzoyan, Ara Mahdessian, and Rev Lebaredian—three people helping open doors for the next generation.

03 / Our story

Built on a life
shared together.

Our partnership began with a shared curiosity about creativity and technology. Living and working across different places taught us how much opportunity depends on the communities around us.

Over the years, Armenia and New Orleans became anchors of our philanthropic work. We created the foundation in 2023 to turn that commitment into sustained action.

We believe in people. We believe in institutions that outlast any one of us. And we believe in showing up for both.

Rev and Rachel

1996

A meeting in Santa Cruz begins a partnership in life, creativity, and technology.

2005

Regular visits to Armenia grow into lasting relationships and a shared commitment.

2023

The foundation brings that work together, with a focus on the next generation.
